#Anchovy references

FISHERIES_2020_JAN_SWG-PEL_02_Data_used_2020_anchovy_assessment.pdf Data record for the 2020 assessment (data 1984-2019). Contains monthly commercial catch tonnage, raised length frequencies, November hydroacoustic survey biomass and CVs, DEPM spawner biomass, recruit survey biomass/numbers/CVs and mean recruit weight. 

FISHERIES_2020_JAN_SWG_PEL_03_South_African_anchovy_assessment_using_data_1984-2019_initial_results.pdf Initial assessment results (1984-2019). Bayesian age-structured model with Beverton-Holt stock-recruitment relationship. Contains estimated natural mortality, von Bertalanffy growth parameters, maturity ogive, survey and commercial selectivity-at-length, weight-at-length relationship, harvest proportions, and the full model specification with prior distributions.

FISHERIES_2021_APR_SWG-PEL_20rev_Anchovy_only_OMP_Operating_Model.pdf Operating model specification for anchovy-only OMP-18rev. Details population dynamics for forward projections, implementation model (TAC/TAB to catches-at-age), observation model, catch allocation assumptions, and historical average weights-at-age.

FISHERIES_2024_AUG_SWG-PEL_34_Anchovy_Data_Record.pdf Data record for the 2024 assessment (data 1984-2023). Updated monthly commercial catch tonnage, November hydroacoustic biomass and CVs (revised time series), DEPM spawner biomass, recruit survey biomass/numbers/CVs and mean recruit weight, and February/March 2024 survey data. Uses revised length-weight relationship.

FISHERIES_2024_AUG_SWG_PEL_35_AnchovyAssessment.pdf Initial benchmark assessment results (1984-2023). Tests alternative stock-recruitment relationships, natural mortality options, time-varying recruitment timing, and time-varying commercial selectivity. Contains updated model specification with Richards/von Bertalanffy growth, annually varying maturity ogives, and full parameter tables with prior distributions.

FISHERIES_2025_AUG_SWG-PEL_47_Final2024AnchovyTAC.pdf OMP-18rev calculated final anchovy TAC for 2025. Contains the input data used (survey biomass, recruitment estimate, recruit and adult catch), correction factors applied to convert the delayed March/April 2025 survey to equivalent November 2024 biomass.

IWS/2025/Sardine/2025/BG7 Assessment_of_the_South_African_anchovy_resource_using_data_up_to_2024.pdf Updated baseline assessment (data 1984-2024). Compares time-invariant natural mortality with time-varying M, density-dependent M, and a two-period Beverton-Holt stock-recruitment relationship. Contains November hydroacoustic biomass and CVs, DEPM spawner biomass, recruit survey numbers and CVs, survey and commercial proportions-at-length, estimated growth curve, maturity ogives, survey and commercial selectivity-at-length, harvest proportions, stock-recruitment relationships and residuals, and the full model specification with prior distributions. 

IWS/2025/Sardine/2025/P2 - IWS_2025_Sardine_P2_Stock_recruitment_relationships_for_South_African_anchovy.pdf Alternative stock-recruitment relationships for the upcoming joint sardine-anchovy MSE. Compares time-invariant and multi-period Beverton-Holt and Hockey-Stick relationships. Contains estimated stock-recruitment curves and residual plots for each alternative, and a likelihood comparison table.

#Sardine references


FISHERIES_2024_OCT_SWG-PEL_40 Sardine Data Record.pdf Documents all data inputs for the 2024 sardine assessment (data through end 2023): commercial catch by area split into directed/large bycatch and small bycatch with anchovy, November survey biomass by area, recruit survey biomass/numbers by area, and parasite prevalence-at-length data used to distinguish CTS/WTS stocks. Recruit cut-off lengths derived from 20% maturity. 

FISHERIES_2025_AUG_SWG-PEL_45 KeySensitivityTestsSardineAssessment.pdf Sardine natural mortality sensitivity tests. Explores fixed M values for juveniles and adults, plus an M increment for fish on their non-preferred coast. To inform baseline and robustness OMs for the upcoming sardine-anchovy MSE. Results given for both no-SRR and Beverton-Holt SRR models.

FISHERIES_2025_JUL_SWG-PEL_43 AlternativeCMPsTowardsAScopingMP.pdf Compares candidate MPs under a Beverton-Holt SRR Operating Model, with variants for bycatch assumptions. TAC set as a fixed proportion of observed west coast biomass. Performance statistics include risk threshold exceedance, average catch, biomass depletion relative to no-catch, and end-of-projection biomass ratios.

FISHERIES_2025_JUN_SWG-PEL_31 SardineAssessmentPosteriorDistributions.pdf. Provides initial MCMC posterior distributions for the two-stock sardine assessment under S0 (no SRR) and SBH (Beverton-Holt SRR). 

FISHERIES_2025_MAY_SWG-PEL_26 SardineAssessment_DataTo2024. Updates the sardine assessment to include the March/April 2025 survey and 2024 commercial data. Appendix details the 2024 data inputs used.

FISHERIES_2025_OCT_SWG-PEL 53 TTGrequests.pdf Responses on sardine SRR and parameter estimation. Provides Hessian-based parameter estimates and standard errors for the updated base case model (SB). Tests Hockey-Stick and Beverton-Holt SRRs fitted after conditioning. 

FISHERIES_2025_SEP_SWG-PEL 49 SardineSensitivityTests.pdf Sensitivity tests for baseline operating model selection. Explores time-varying and density-dependent natural mortality, parasite prevalence-at-length data, and alternative catch-by-stock weightings off the west coast. Lists candidate reference set models and robustness tests for the joint sardine-anchovy MSE.

FISHERIES_2026_APR_SWG-PEL_13 AdditionalTestingOfBetaPriors.pdf Testing of alternative beta prior distributions for proportional split of total recruitment by stock and coast. Recommends preferred prior distributions for the proportion of WTS recruitment and proportion recruiting to the west coast.

IWS_2025_Sardine_BG4 - Assessment results (data 1984-2024) for two model variants: independently estimated recruitment (SnoSR) and with stock-recruitment relationships. Contains estimated biomass, recruitment, spawner biomass, growth curves, standard deviation about mean length-at-age, survey and commercial selectivity-at-length, movement proportions, and harvest proportions by coast.

IWS_2025_Sardine_BG5 Scoping MSE for South African sardine.pdf Details the Operating Model used for the scoping MSE for sardine, based mostly on the updated assessment of the revised two stock hypothesis. 

IWS_2025_Sardine_BG3 - Revisions updated assessment_model_revised_sardine_stock_structure_hypothesis.pdf
Full model specification for the revised two-stock (CTS/WTS), two-area (west/south coast) sardine assessment. Details population dynamics equations, coast-dependent growth, active movement modelling, recruitment parameterisation, maturity, selectivity, and likelihood formulation. 

IWS_2025_Sardine_P3 - Twenty-year projections of the sardine population using the Scoping MSE. Tests two non-parametric future recruitment approaches: historical median and Hockey-Stick-like relationship. Examines impact of recruitment variability on projections with and without future catch.
